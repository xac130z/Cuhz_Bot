# Planet CUHZ: Brand Identity & Capability Inventory

## 🌌 Brand Identity

**Core Mission:** To build the most welcoming creator community and provide AI-powered tools for streamers, gamers, and creators.
**Tagline:** "From 'cousin' to 'cuhz', join the most welcoming creator community."
**Aesthetic:** "Cosmic family," futuristic, high-energy, and creator-centric.
**Tone:** Friendly, inclusive ("Cuhz"), but technically advanced (AI-first).

### Visual Elements
- **Logo:** Glowing celestial chain/pendant.
- **Colors:** Electric Cyan, Electric Blue, Deep Space Purple/Magenta.
- **Key Motifs:** Stars, galaxies, futuristic chains, and 2K-inspired UI elements.

## 🛠 Capability Inventory

Planet CUHZ provides a range of tools and community features categorized across three main pillars:

### 1. AI-Powered Gaming Tools (2K Protocol)
- **AI Build Assistant:** Optimizes NBA 2K character builds using AI analysis.
- **Squad Finder:**
  - **Basic:** Manual discovery (Free).
  - **Premium:** Chemistry-based matching + auto-crew formation (Premium).
- **Crew Progress Tracker:** Monitor performance and growth of gaming crews.
- **Rep Optimization Alerts:** Targeted notifications for maximizing in-game reputation gains.
- **Daily Grind Digest:** Automated summaries of gaming sessions and progress.

### 2. Streamer & Creator Ecosystem (Antigravity Agent)
- **Twitch Bot Integration:** Automated engagement, marketing timers, and "Stream Aware" functionality.
- **Smart Mode:** Stream status detection for automated messaging.
- **Dynamic Help & Commands:** Dashboard-controlled command sets.
- **Point & Reward System:** Community engagement tracking (💎 CUHZ points).
- **Moderation Suite:** Standardized tools for announcements, raids, and chat control.

### 3. Community & Competitive
- **Tournament of Power:** Tiered competitive environment for the community.
- **Tournament tiers:** 11 levels of "Power," ranging from trainees to galactic warriors.
- **Coaching Hub:** Access to live coaches and strategy sessions.
- **Communication:** Integrated Discord and PSN group chats for real-time networking.
